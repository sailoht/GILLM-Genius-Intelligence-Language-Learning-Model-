# GILLM & LangBaby Architecture Specification

```text
Human
  ↕
LangBaby (Communication Subsystem)
  ↕
GIR (Genius Intelligence Representation)
  ↕
GILLM Intelligence Core
  ↕
World Model / 3D Registry / Executable Laws
```

---

## 1. LangBaby (Communication Subsystem)

LangBaby is the human communication subsystem inside GILLM. It handles:
*   `Human Language` $\rightarrow$ `Language Structure` $\rightarrow$ `GIR`
*   `GIR` $\rightarrow$ `Semantic Structure` $\rightarrow$ `Language Construction` $\rightarrow$ `Human Language`

LangBaby is strictly a communication channel and does **not** serve as the intelligence core.

---

## 2. GILLM Intelligence Core

GILLM is the complete intelligence architecture, responsible for:
*   World modeling and spatial 3D Information Registry
*   Information representation via DataAtoms and DataMolecules
*   3D spatial search, filtering, and operator pipelines
*   Executable Laws (`LawRegistry`) and dimensional unit checks
*   Proof-carrying state synthesis and provenance tracking
*   Structured 13-stage Investigation Loop
*   Epistemic and validation status tracking
